# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and protect private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible.
